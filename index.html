About Me

I am Rohan Singhal, an 18-year-old student from Pune, currently living in Worli, Mumbai. I am someone who likes to take charge, figure things out on my own, and get things done rather than waiting around. Right now, I am doing two degrees at the same time—a regular BBA at Jai Hind College in Mumbai and an online BBA in Digital Business and Entrepreneurship from IIM Bangalore—because my main goal is to build real-world business skills and start earning early, not just collect degrees. I apply the same discipline to my fitness—I work out regularly and follow a strict dairy-free, high-protein diet. Right now, I am also looking for part-time jobs or internships in Mumbai in sales, marketing, HR, or customer service to get real work experience, and I prefer keeping my profile 100% real without fake resume filler.
